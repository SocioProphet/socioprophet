# Marketer-Safe Outputs and Segment Proofs

Entity Analytics does not need to export the whole person to be useful.

## Marketer-safe outputs

The system can emit:
- coarse cohorts
- bounded summaries
- topic mixtures
- time-windowed signals
- proof artifacts about what was and was not exported

## Why proofs matter

The platform should be able to show:
- which inputs contributed
- which contexts were excluded
- what coarsening or suppression rules were applied
- why the output remained policy-safe

## Product implication

This surface is useful for outreach and measurement without turning the platform into identity extraction infrastructure.
