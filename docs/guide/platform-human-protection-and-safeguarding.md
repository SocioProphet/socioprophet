# Platform Human Protection and Safeguarding

SocioProphet is designed in favor of protecting humans first.

This is not a decorative principle. It is the operating rule for the platform.

## Core commitments

The platform protects:

- human agency over machine substitution
- minors and vulnerable participants over convenience
- traceability over opaque automation
- bounded capability over unrestricted execution
- recovery and escalation over silent failure

## Platform defaults

SocioProphet defaults to:

- least privilege
- role-aware access
- explicit capability gating
- visible recovery states
- attributable actions
- documented escalation paths
- safer defaults for minors, guardians, and institutions

## Human-first design rule

Humans decide ends, approvals, and legitimacy.

Machines assist with bounded means under visible policy, evidence, and audit constraints.

## Minor-safe design rule

Minor access is never treated as a normal low-friction consumer flow.

Minor participation is governed by:
- guardian-linked or institution-governed participation
- explicit capability restrictions
- stronger reporting and escalation pathways
- elevated consent and review expectations

## Surface implications

Every first-class surface must explain:
- who it is for
- what trust boundary it crosses
- what safety restrictions apply
- what recovery and escalation path exists

This is part of the product contract, not optional documentation.
