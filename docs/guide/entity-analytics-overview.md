# Entity Analytics Overview

Entity Analytics is a first-class SocioProphet surface for identity-aware, policy-constrained, proof-producing analytics.

It starts from typed events, scoped features, evidence, and governed identity operations rather than blind warehouse linkage.

## What it does

- builds entity views from typed event streams
- preserves relationship edges instead of flattening everything into one irreversible profile
- constrains merge behavior with policy, not confidence alone
- emits proofs and marketer-safe outputs
- integrates with digital trust, auth, organizations, and safeguarding rules

## Why this matters

This surface sits at the junction of:
- identity
- digital trust
- provider capability routing
- institutional governance
- guardian and minor protections
- safe export and segment formation
