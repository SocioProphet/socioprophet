# Digital Trust and Capability Routing

The `.digital` surface is not only about presentation. It is about trusted digital presence tied to identity, capability, and recovery.

## Digital trust model

Digital trust includes:

- identity legibility
- reputation and continuity
- privacy-aware visibility
- traceable capability attachment
- recoverable connection states

## Capability routing

Digital surfaces connect directly to the auth and connections story.

A public-facing digital surface can route users toward:
- platform sign-in
- provider connection state
- capability health
- recovery and degraded-state handling

## Why this matters

A digital presence without governed capability routing is just branding.

SocioProphet treats digital trust as the connection between:
- who the user is
- what they are allowed to do
- what capabilities are connected
- how failure is handled
