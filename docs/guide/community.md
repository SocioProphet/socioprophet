# Community and Support

SocioProphet uses **Sociosphere on Matrix** as a live community coordination surface.

## Canonical Matrix space

- **Matrix space:** [Open Sociosphere on Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/!KXodmWaRcnATXHvxCn:matrix.org/$U-VdMKyieO3_zdf9AXZmdS0-f_FvcoEiNRWp07utmPc?via=matrix.org)

## What it is for

Sociosphere is the coordination surface for:

- community participation
- contributor onboarding
- product and documentation discussion
- early support and troubleshooting
- collaboration across the ecosystem

## Support model

For now, Matrix serves as the primary live support and coordination channel while broader support tooling is brought online.

Use it for:

- asking questions
- coordinating contributions
- finding the right documentation path
- joining project discussions
- interacting with operators and AI-assisted guidance

## Trust boundary model

The platform is a trust mesh. Participation, visibility, and access are shaped by trust boundaries and role.

At a high level, SocioProphet serves:

- Humans
- Organizations
- Institutions

The Matrix space is the first live community surface connecting those layers through public collaboration and progressive trust.

## Documentation alignment

The Matrix space should be used alongside:

- the main documentation
- the wiki / knowledge commons
- support and onboarding flows
- contributor guidance and process docs

## Notes

As the platform evolves, Matrix can remain the community coordination layer while additional support and operational surfaces are added around it.
