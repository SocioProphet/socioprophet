# Liberty by Design — SocioProphet

This document is intentionally **product-scoped**.
It defines the constitutional commitments that should govern SocioProphet as a knowledge, governance, education, and evidence system for people and institutions.

Cross-repo workspace doctrine, OS/runtime substrate doctrine, and agent-plane orchestration doctrine should live outside this repo, primarily in `SocioProphet/sociosphere`.

---

## 1) The product problem: epistemic capture inside institutional systems

Most institutional software does not fail because it lacks data. It fails because it turns data into a bottleneck, hides reasoning, obscures provenance, and makes challenge expensive.

A product becomes dangerous when it silently converts:
- evidence into authority theater
- dashboards into unquestionable truth
- governance into invisible enforcement
- AI assistance into unappealable output

SocioProphet should resist that shape.

The product thesis is simple: **understanding must remain distributed, challengeable, and replayable**.

---

## 2) Evidence before authority

SocioProphet should not ask users to trust claims because a prestigious actor, internal workflow, or model produced them.
It should structure claims so they can be inspected, challenged, and replayed.

That means product surfaces should favor:
- provenance over assertion
- evidence pointers over summary theater
- traceable diffs over silent mutation
- replayable workflows over black-box outcomes

The enemy is not disagreement. The enemy is non-inspectable power.

---

## 3) Privacy for persons, transparency for power

This repo’s product doctrine should preserve a hard asymmetry:
- **persons deserve privacy**
- **power deserves transparency**

In product terms, that means:
- intentional publication boundaries
- audited access to sensitive material
- selective disclosure when possible
- visible policy for restricted operations
- durable traces for consequential institutional decisions

If the product flips this asymmetry, it becomes a surveillance instrument wearing a governance costume. That costume is clown shoes with a security badge.

---

## 4) Education as a first-class product function

SocioProphet should not merely deliver conclusions. It should improve the user’s ability to inspect, interpret, and challenge those conclusions.

Product features should therefore support:
- explanation
- replay
- comparison
- correction
- curricular propagation
- institutional memory

A healthy product increases human capability per unit of machine assistance.
A bad product increases dependency per unit of machine assistance.

---

## 5) Accountable machine assistance

Machine assistance is allowed inside this doctrine only when it remains bounded, attributable, and reviewable.

In product scope, this means:
- AI outputs should carry evidence context where possible
- consequential actions should expose inputs, constraints, and policy context
- denials should be explainable and appealable
- moderation/governance actions should leave structured traces
- replay should be available for critical workflows

The product may assist judgment. It must not counterfeit legitimacy.

---

## 6) Governance must be visible and appealable

Invisible enforcement is the authoritarian bug pattern of software.

SocioProphet product surfaces should therefore make governance legible:
- what rule applied
- what evidence was used
- what action occurred
- what changed
- how to appeal or challenge

Default-deny is acceptable when paired with visible reasons, evidence pointers, and a structured appeal path.
Silence plus coercion is not governance. It is productized priestcraft.

---

## 7) Product invariants

The following invariants should be treated as acceptance criteria for SocioProphet product surfaces:

- No consequential action without evidence.
- No durable claim without provenance.
- No governance action without visibility.
- No denial without an appeal path.
- No replayable-critical workflow without a replay recipe or equivalent audit surface.
- No publication of personal material without explicit policy basis.
- No silent mutation of institutional truth.
- No AI summary that cannot be challenged against underlying evidence.

If these invariants fail, liberty becomes branding copy instead of system behavior.

---

## 8) What belongs elsewhere

This repo is not the right home for every neighboring doctrine.
The following topics should primarily live in `SocioProphet/sociosphere` or another infra/workspace repo when they are cross-repo rather than product-specific:

- workspace controller doctrine
- OS/runtime substrate doctrine
- agent-plane orchestration doctrine
- repo composition / materialization doctrine
- transport/wire/runtime capability doctrine when not directly product-facing

That boundary matters because repo ontology is part of governance. If everything lives everywhere, nothing is actually governed.
