# Privacy Boundaries v1 (Spec Stub)

Transparency for power; privacy for persons.

## Mechanisms
- commitments + hashes in public logs; payload disclosure by policy
- selective disclosure credentials
- tiered logs: public metadata, restricted payload access under audited policy
- local-first cognition; publish only intentional artifacts

## Auditability
- all access to restricted payloads is itself logged and replayable
