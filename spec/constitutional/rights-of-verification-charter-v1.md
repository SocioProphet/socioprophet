# Rights of Verification Charter v1

This is the machine-checkable constitutional layer that turns "liberty by design" into enforceable acceptance criteria.

## Rights (for humans / citizens / participants)
- Inspect inputs and provenance for critical outputs.
- Inspect constraints, policies, and governance diffs.
- Replay critical outcomes locally (or via reproducible remote runner).
- Challenge claims with counter-evidence and demand a replay.
- Fork the commons without losing history/provenance.

## Duties (for systems / services / agents)
- Attach attestations to artifacts and decisions.
- Publish transparency log pointers for critical events.
- Provide replay recipes and denial reasons with evidence pointers.
- Provide an appeal path with structured evidence.
- Default-deny on missing or invalid proof.

## Non-negotiable invariants
- No action without evidence.
- No artifact without provenance.
- No authority without verifiability.
- No enforcement without visibility + appeal.
- Privacy for persons; transparency for power.
