This short intake helps us route people into the right Academy path: learner, family, mentor, organization, or future opportunity-side. Academy is not a content funnel. It is a serious learning surface, and this page helps us understand the right place to begin.

Why we ask

Some people arrive looking for a K–12 or family learning path. Some want lifelong systems education. Some want to mentor, run cohorts, deploy Academy in an organization, or understand future opportunity pathways. This intake helps us route each person into the right lane.

Who this is for

Learners & Families — serious study, homeschool, and long-form learning paths.
Mentors & Educators — cohort guidance, learner support, and teaching paths.
Organizations — schools, nonprofits, public-interest programs, and deployment conversations.
Opportunity side — future capability, placement, and ethical work-path routing.

What happens next

We use this intake to route follow-up appropriately. That may mean reading materials, a cohort-fit conversation, a mentor path, or an organizational deployment discussion. This is a routing mechanism, not a commitment.
