Institutions & Sovereign Systems

For public sector, sovereign, and mission-critical environments that require secure learning, training, coordination, and knowledge systems. This lane supports public service, civil preparedness, resilience, and high-trust operational contexts without abandoning the commons-first ethos of the platform.

Human Safeguards and Defensive Perimeter

We support sovereign, public-sector, and mission-critical deployments only where the mission is human protection, public readiness, secure learning, resilience, and ethical systems operation. Systems inside our authorized defensive perimeter may be actively hardened and defended. Systems outside that perimeter may be monitored and assessed for risk using lawful, non-destructive methods, but not penetrated without authorization. Anti-human use cases are refused, quarantined, and escalated.

High-risk and charter-sensitive functions remain under founder control while proof-of-life and capacity conditions are satisfied. Anti-human directives are refused. Founder refusal is final.
